Our Story

From Thiruvalla to all of South Kerala – making solar simple and affordable

Solis Green India started its journey in Thiruvalla with a clear vision: to help Kerala move towards clean, reliable and affordable solar energy. What began as a small, passionate team has grown into a trusted solar company serving multiple districts across South Kerala.

Since 2019, we have designed and installed solar power plants for independent homes, apartments, shops, hospitals, educational institutions and small industries. Every project is carefully engineered to match the customer's load, budget and roof conditions.

Today, with 500+ completed projects and 95% customer satisfaction, Solis Green India is known for transparent pricing, honest guidance on government subsidies and dependable after-sales service.

We serve key towns like Thiruvalla, Ranni, Adoor, Pathanamthitta, Changanacherry, Pala, Kanjirapally, Cherthala, Haripad, Kayamkulam, Mavelikkara and many more – with local teams ready to support you quickly.
