Our Story

From vision to reality - transforming Kerala's energy landscape

Founded with a vision to transform Kerala's energy landscape, Solis Green India emerged as a pioneer in sustainable energy solutions.

What started as a small venture in Pathanamthitta has grown into one of South Kerala's most trusted solar energy providers. Our journey began with a simple belief: every home and business in Kerala deserves access to clean, affordable, and reliable solar energy.

Today, we've successfully completed 500+ solar installations across multiple districts, helping families and businesses reduce their electricity bills by up to 90% while contributing to a cleaner environment.
