Our Story

Solis Green India began its journey in Thiruvalla with a simple mission: to make solar energy accessible, reliable, and affordable for every home and business in Kerala.

What started as a small team of passionate engineers in 2019 has grown into one of Kerala's most trusted solar companies, with hundreds of successful installations across multiple districts.

Our journey is built on trust, technical excellence, and a deep commitment to customer satisfaction. We believe in solar energy not just as a business, but as a way to power Kerala's sustainable future.

2019
Founded in Thiruvalla with a focus on quality residential solar installations
2020
Expanded to commercial solar projects and established local teams
2021
Crossed 200 installations and expanded service to Kottayam & Alappuzha
2023
Reached 500+ installations and introduced comprehensive AMC services

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide every home and business in Kerala with reliable, affordable, and sustainable solar energy solutions that reduce electricity costs and carbon footprint.

We aim to make the transition to solar energy simple, transparent, and rewarding for our customers through expert guidance and quality installations.

Our Vision

To be Kerala's most trusted solar energy partner, powering a sustainable future through innovative solar solutions and exceptional customer service.

We envision a Kerala where clean, renewable energy is accessible to all, contributing to energy independence and environmental preservation.
