How to Register for PM Surya Ghar in Kerala (Step-by-Step)

As an approved MNRE vendor, Solis Green Energy Solutions guides you through every step or executes the entire filing on your behalf:

1

Registration on pmsuryaghar.gov.in

We submit your 13-digit KSEB consumer number, electrical section name, mobile number (linked with Aadhaar), and email address on the National Rooftop Solar Portal.

2

KSEB reCap Feasibility Approval

Your local KSEB Assistant Engineer reviews local distribution transformer capacity via the reCap system. Technical feasibility approval is typically granted within 3 to 7 working days.

3

Turnkey Solar Plant Installation by Solis Green

Our certified technicians install Tier-1 ALMM & DCR certified mono-PERC or TOPCon bifacial modules, an IP66 on-grid string inverter, hot-dip galvanized mounting structures, and multi-point earthing with lightning arresters.

4

KSEB Inspection & Bi-directional Net Metering

KSEB electrical sub-division inspectors visit your premises, conduct synchronization testing, replace your existing energy meter with an import-export bi-directional net meter, and issue the commissioning certificate.

5

Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) Subsidy Credit

We upload the final commissioning report and plant photograph to the portal. The MNRE releases your ₹78,000 subsidy directly into your bank account within 30 days!

Frequently Asked Questions – PM Surya Ghar Kerala

How much subsidy is provided under PM Surya Ghar Yojana in Kerala?

Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, Kerala residential consumers receive direct bank transfer (DBT) subsidies: ₹33,000 for 1kW, ₹66,000 for 2kW, and a flat ₹78,000 for 3kW and higher systems.

How is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy claimed in Kerala?

Solis Green registers your 13-digit KSEB consumer number on pmsuryaghar.gov.in and KSEB's reCap portal. Once the plant is commissioned and the bi-directional net meter is installed, MNRE disburses the ₹78,000 directly into your bank account within 30 days.

Can I get an SBI solar loan for PM Surya Ghar in Kerala?

Yes. Nationalized banks including State Bank of India (SBI) and Canara Bank provide collateral-free rooftop solar loans at special interest rates around 6% to 7% per annum for up to 10 years.

What are the DCR requirements for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

To receive the Central subsidy, the solar panels must be Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) certified, manufactured with solar cells and modules made in India and approved under the ALMM list. Solis Green exclusively supplies Tier-1 DCR ALMM modules.

Is PM Surya Ghar subsidy available for commercial buildings or shops?

No. The ₹78,000 central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar is strictly for residential individual houses, villas, and registered residential housing societies. Commercial and industrial properties can instead claim 40% Accelerated Depreciation tax benefits.
