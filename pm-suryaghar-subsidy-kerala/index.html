Frequently Asked Questions — PM Surya Ghar Kerala

How do I register for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Registration requires your 13-digit KSEB consumer number and Aadhaar-linked mobile phone. Solis Green handles the online portal filing, site survey reports, and KSEB feasibility clearances on your behalf.

When is the subsidy deposited into my account?

After your solar plant is erected, KSEB tests and installs the bi-directional net meter. Solis Green uploads the final commissioning report and photos. The MNRE disburses the subsidy directly into your bank account within 30 days.

What are the DCR requirements for subsidy?

To qualify for the Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy, solar panels must be Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) certified, manufactured with cells and modules made in India. Solis Green uses only Tier-1 ALMM & DCR certified panels (Adani, Waaree, Tata).
