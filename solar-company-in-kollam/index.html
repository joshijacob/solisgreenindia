Solar Energy Adoption in Kollam District

Kollam district is witnessing rapid growth in rooftop solar adoption due to rising residential electricity consumption and increasing awareness about long-term savings through net-metering.

Coastal humidity and salt exposure demand proper engineering execution. Using corrosion-resistant aluminium structures, certified inverters, and structured earthing protection ensures system durability.

Our EPC approach includes site inspection, structural assessment, system sizing, MNRE subsidy processing, KSEB approvals, installation and post-installation technical support.
