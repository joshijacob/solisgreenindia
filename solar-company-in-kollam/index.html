Frequently Asked Questions – Kollam Solar

How much subsidy can I get in Kollam under PM Surya Ghar?

Residential consumers receive ₹33,000 for 1kW, ₹66,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW or larger systems. Solis Green is an approved vendor on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal and handles all feasibility documentation directly with KSEB Electrical Circles across Kollam.

Can cashew factories and commercial sheds install solar panels without roof leakage?

Yes! We use specialized standing-seam and non-penetrating aluminum clamps that anchor firmly onto metal shed roofs without drilling a single hole, ensuring 100% weatherproofing and zero rainwater leakage.

How long does KSEB net-metering take in Kollam district?

Feasibility approval through the reCap portal usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Once the solar plant is installed, KSEB electrical inspectors test the bi-directional meter and synchronize the system within 7 to 14 days.
