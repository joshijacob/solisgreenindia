Why Install Rooftop Solar in Kerala?

With high electricity tariffs and abundant year-round sunshine (except during heavy monsoons), Kerala is one of the best states in India for rooftop solar power generation.

Whether you have a 3BHK independent home, an IT office in Trivandrum, or a cashew processing unit in Kollam, switching to solar protects you from rising KSEB bills and provides incredible ROI.
