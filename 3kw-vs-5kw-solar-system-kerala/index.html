3kW vs 5kW Solar System in Kerala: Which One Should You Choose?

Choosing between a 3kW and 5kW on-grid rooftop solar plant depends on your bi-monthly KSEB electricity bill, air conditioning load, and available roof shadow-free area. Here is a direct breakdown under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy scheme.

Feature / Metric 3kW On-Grid Solar 5kW On-Grid Solar
Daily Energy Output 12 – 14 Units / day 20 – 24 Units / day
Bi-Monthly KSEB Bill Target ₹2,500 – ₹5,000 ₹5,500 – ₹10,000+
Central Govt Subsidy ₹78,000 (Max) ₹78,000 (Max)
Roof Area Required Approx. 250 – 300 sq.ft Approx. 400 – 500 sq.ft
Recommended AC Units 1 to 2 Inverter ACs 3 to 4 ACs + EV Charger
Payback Period ~3.2 Years ~3.5 Years

Our Recommendation:

For standard 3-bedroom independent houses in Kerala consuming around 600–700 units bi-monthly, a 3kW system provides the highest financial ROI because you receive the maximum ₹78,000 subsidy on the lowest initial investment. If you have 3+ air conditioners or an Electric Vehicle, go for 5kW.
