Frequently Asked Questions – Kottayam Solar

How much solar subsidy can I get in Kottayam under PM Surya Ghar?

Residential consumers receive ₹33,000 for 1kW, ₹66,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW and higher systems. Solis Green is an approved vendor on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal and handles all feasibility documentation directly with KSEB Electrical Circles across Kottayam.

Can homes in rubber plantation areas install solar panels?

Yes. Many homes in Pala, Kanjirappally, and Erattupetta are surrounded by rubber trees. We conduct 3D sun-path shadow simulations and utilize half-cut bifacial modules or Enphase microinverters so shaded modules do not bottleneck full system generation.

How long does KSEB net-metering take in Kottayam district?

Feasibility approval through the reCap portal usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Once the solar plant is installed, KSEB electrical inspectors test the bi-directional meter and synchronize the system within 7 to 14 days.

What warranties and service support are provided?

You receive a 25-to-30-year performance warranty on solar panels, a 5-to-10-year inverter warranty, and 5 years of complimentary local Annual Maintenance (AMC) backed by our nearby Thiruvalla headquarters.
