Rooftop Solar Installation in Kottayam District – Complete Guide

Kottayam district has emerged as one of Kerala’s fastest-growing regions for rooftop solar adoption. Rising electricity tariffs, dependable sunlight, and strong government subsidy support have made solar power a practical long-term investment for homes and businesses.

Most residential buildings in Kottayam feature RCC or tiled roofs, both suitable for on-grid solar installations. However, factors such as shadow-free area, roof orientation, and local KSEB transformer capacity significantly impact system sizing and approval timelines. A professional site survey is essential to avoid generation loss and net-metering delays.
