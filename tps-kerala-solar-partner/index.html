PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Guide

The Government of India has launched the PM Surya Ghar scheme to promote sustainable energy adoption among residential consumers. As a registered vendor, we provide end-to-end assistance in securing your Solar Subsidy Kerala.

Government Subsidy Structure

Under the current guidelines, residential homeowners can receive substantial financial aid:

  • Up to 2kW: Subsidy of ₹30,000 per kW.
  • Up to 3kW: Additional ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, bringing the total subsidy to ₹78,000.
  • Above 3kW: The maximum subsidy is capped at ₹78,000.

Eligibility and Application Process

To be eligible, you must be a residential consumer with an active electricity connection. The process is entirely digital via the National Portal. Solis Green Energy assists you in registering, selecting us as your vendor, submitting technical feasibility requests to KSEB, and uploading the final commissioning reports required for direct bank transfer of the subsidy amount.

If you require financing, we also offer guidance on accessing low-interest Solar Loan Kerala options through partner banks like SBI and Union Bank, ensuring your transition to solar is incredibly affordable.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the TPS Kerala Solar Partner program?

The TPS Kerala Solar Partner program connects residents and businesses in Kerala with certified, high-quality solar installers like Solis Green Energy Solutions to ensure reliable, efficient, and subsidized solar power systems.

2. How much subsidy can I get through PM Surya Ghar in Kerala?

Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, residential customers can receive a subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for a 3kW system, directly credited to their bank accounts after successful installation and commissioning.

3. Do you provide solar installations in Thiruvalla and Pathanamthitta?

Yes, we provide comprehensive solar installations, maintenance, and consultation services across Thiruvalla, Pathanamthitta, Kottayam, Alappuzha, Kollam, and surrounding districts.

4. What is Net Metering in Kerala?

Net Metering is a billing mechanism provided by KSEB. It credits solar energy system owners for the electricity they add to the grid. If you generate more power than you use, the excess is exported, and you receive credits on your next bill.

5. Is a Hybrid Solar System better than On-Grid?

It depends on your needs. On-Grid systems are best for reducing bills and require the grid to operate. Hybrid systems include batteries to provide backup during power cuts, making them ideal for areas with frequent outages.

6. Do commercial buildings get the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

No, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy is exclusively for residential consumers. However, commercial entities benefit greatly from accelerated depreciation (tax benefits) and a massive reduction in operational utility costs.

7. How long does the installation process take?

The physical installation of the panels usually takes 2-4 days. However, the complete process, including KSEB feasibility approvals and net meter commissioning, typically takes 3 to 6 weeks depending on local grid office processing times.

8. What maintenance does a solar system require?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance. The primary requirement is regular cleaning of the solar panels (every 2-4 weeks) to remove dust and bird droppings, ensuring maximum sunlight absorption.

9. How durable are the solar panels during Kerala monsoons?

Tier-1 solar panels are highly durable and designed to withstand heavy rain, strong winds, and hail. Our mounting structures are heavily galvanized to prevent rust in Kerala's humid climate.

10. Can I get a loan to install a solar system?

Yes, several banks including SBI and Union Bank offer dedicated low-interest Solar Loans for residential systems under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. We provide full documentation assistance for loan approvals.

11. Will a solar system power my air conditioner?

Yes. A properly sized on-grid solar system will offset the heavy electricity consumption of air conditioners. We will analyze your load to recommend the correct kW capacity.

12. Are Micro Inverters better than String Inverters?

Micro inverters optimize each panel individually, making them superior for roofs with partial shading. They are generally more expensive but provide better overall yield in complex installations.

13. Do you service areas like Konni and Ranni?

Absolutely. Our installation and maintenance teams frequently service Konni, Ranni, Mallappally, and other interior regions of Pathanamthitta and Kottayam districts.

14. What happens if the grid fails with an On-Grid system?

For safety reasons (anti-islanding), an On-Grid inverter automatically shuts down during a grid failure to protect linemen repairing the grid. If you need backup, you must opt for a Hybrid system.

15. What warranties do you offer?

We provide 25-year performance warranties on solar panels, 5 to 10-year warranties on inverters (depending on the brand), and a standard workmanship warranty on our installations.

16. Can solar panels be installed on flat concrete roofs and sloped tile roofs?

Yes. We design custom mounting structures for flat concrete (RCC) roofs, sloped sheet roofs, and traditional Kerala tile roofs to ensure secure, leak-proof installations.

17. How can I monitor my solar panel's generation?

All modern inverters we install come with Wi-Fi connectivity. We will set up an app on your smartphone that allows you to monitor real-time generation, daily yields, and historical data.

18. Is there an inspection required by KSEB?

Yes. Once installation is complete, a KSEB official must inspect the site to ensure it meets safety standards before authorizing the net meter installation.

19. Why should I choose Solis over local electricians?

Solar is a highly technical field requiring electrical engineering expertise. We ensure correct wire sizing, proper earthing, lightning protection, and structural safety that untrained electricians cannot guarantee.
