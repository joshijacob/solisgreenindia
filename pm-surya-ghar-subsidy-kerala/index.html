What is the PM Surya Ghar Scheme?

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government initiative designed to light up 1 crore households across India by providing up to 300 units of free electricity every month. By installing a rooftop solar system on your Kerala home, you can significantly reduce your KSEB bills and earn from surplus power generation.

1kW to 2kW Systems

Ideal for homes with monthly electricity bills between ₹1,000 and ₹2,000.

  • Subsidy: ₹30,000 per kW
  • Max Subsidy: ₹60,000

3kW Systems (Most Popular)

Ideal for homes looking to completely eliminate a ₹2,500 - ₹3,000 monthly bill.

  • Subsidy: Fixed at ₹78,000
  • ROI: Typically within 3-4 years

Above 3kW Systems

For larger homes with heavy loads like multiple air conditioners.

  • Subsidy: Capped at ₹78,000
  • Benefit: Maximum generation and potential to sell back to KSEB.

How to Register on the National Portal

Solis Green Energy Solutions is an MNRE-empanelled vendor. We handle the entire process for you!

1

Registration at pmsuryaghar.gov.in

We help you register on the portal using your State (Kerala), DISCOM (KSEB), Consumer Number, Mobile, and Email.

2

Apply for Rooftop Solar

Login with your credentials and apply. Wait for KSEB to provide the technical feasibility approval.

3

Installation by Solis Green

Once feasibility is approved, we (your registered vendor) will install the complete system as per MNRE standards.

4

Net Metering & Inspection

Submit plant details on the portal. KSEB officials will inspect the site and install the net meter.

5

Direct Bank Transfer (DBT)

Upload your bank account details and a cancelled cheque. The subsidy will hit your account within 30 days!
