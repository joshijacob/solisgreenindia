MNRE Solar Subsidy in Kottayam

Residential on-grid solar systems in Kottayam are eligible for PM Surya Ghar / MNRE subsidy. Approval depends on correct documentation and KSEB net-metering clearance.

Eligibility Based

Subsidy applies mainly for residential on-grid systems.

KSEB Approval

Transformer capacity plays a key role in approval timelines.

Installer Responsibility

Correct MNRE process avoids subsidy rejection.
