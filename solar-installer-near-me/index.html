Ensure the installer is an MNRE empaneled vendor and KSEB approved with physical branch operations in your district. Always ask for real customer reviews, local office addresses, and avoid fly-by-night operators who outsource installation to third-party laborers.

Does Solis Green provide free rooftop site surveys near me?

Who handles KSEB net metering and transformer feasibility checks?

Solis Green takes end-to-end responsibility. We submit the online application on KSEB's reCap portal, pay feasibility fees, submit work completion test reports, and coordinate with the local Assistant Engineer for bi-directional net meter commissioning.

What is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy available near me?

The Central Government provides a flat ₹33,000 subsidy for 1kW, ₹66,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW and higher residential systems. We handle all portal documentation to ensure rapid direct bank transfer credit.
