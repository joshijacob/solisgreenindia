Solar Maintenance Tips

Regular Cleaning

Clean panels every 3-6 months for optimal performance

  • Use soft cloth and water
  • Avoid abrasive materials
  • Clean early morning or evening

Visual Inspection

Monthly visual checks can prevent major issues

  • Check for physical damage
  • Look for bird droppings
  • Inspect for shading

Monitor Performance

Track your system's daily energy production

  • Use monitoring apps
  • Compare with previous months
  • Note any sudden drops

Professional Help

When to call professionals

  • Error messages on inverter
  • Significant efficiency drop
  • Physical damage to panels

Maintenance FAQs

How often should I clean my solar panels?

We recommend professional cleaning every 3-6 months. In dusty areas or during dry seasons, more frequent cleaning may be needed.

Can I clean solar panels myself?

While basic cleaning is possible, professional cleaning ensures safety, uses proper equipment, and doesn't void warranties.

What affects solar panel efficiency?

Dust accumulation, bird droppings, shading, panel age, and weather conditions can significantly impact efficiency.

Do you provide warranty on repairs?

Yes, we provide 6 months warranty on all repairs and 1 year warranty on replaced components.

How quickly can you respond to emergency calls?

We guarantee 2-4 hour response time for emergency repairs in major cities and 4-8 hours in other areas.

What's included in AMC?

AMC includes regular cleaning, inspections, performance monitoring, priority support, and repair discounts.
