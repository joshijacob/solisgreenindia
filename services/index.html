Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our solar services

What is included in the 5-year FREE AMC?

Our 5-year FREE AMC includes: bi-annual professional site visits, daily remote system monitoring, all genuine fault repairs at zero cost, replacement parts when needed, performance optimization, and 24/7 emergency support. Everything is completely FREE for 5 years.

How much government subsidy can I get for residential solar?

For residential solar systems, you can get up to 40% subsidy from the government. For a 3kW system, this means approximately ₹45,000-₹50,000 subsidy. We provide complete assistance with subsidy application and paperwork.

How long does solar installation take?

Residential installations typically take 3-5 days, commercial projects 1-2 weeks, and industrial installations 2-4 weeks. This includes KSEB approval process and commissioning.

Do you provide battery backup systems?

Yes, we offer both on-grid systems (without battery) and hybrid systems (with battery backup). Battery systems are recommended for areas with frequent power cuts and provide uninterrupted power supply.

What is the payback period for solar investment?

Typically 3-4 years for residential systems, 2-3 years for commercial, and 3-4 years for industrial systems. After the payback period, you get virtually free electricity for 20+ years.
