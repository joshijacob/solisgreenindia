Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save with solar?

Most homeowners save 70-80% on their electricity bills. A typical 3kW system can save ₹4,000-₹6,000 per month.

How long does installation take?

Complete installation takes 3-5 days after subsidy approval. We ensure minimal disruption to your daily life.

What maintenance is required?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance - just occasional cleaning. We provide free first-year maintenance.

Will it work during power cuts?

Yes, with battery backup. Without batteries, it works only when grid power is available (safety feature).

Is my roof suitable for solar?

Most concrete, tiled, and metal sheet roofs are suitable. Our free site survey will confirm compatibility.

What about rainy seasons?

Solar panels work efficiently in Kerala's climate. They generate power even on cloudy days, though slightly less.
