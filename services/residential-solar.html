Frequently Asked Questions

What is the KSEB 90% transformer limit?

KSEB regulation states solar installations cannot exceed 90% of local transformer capacity. Once reached, no new solar allowed until transformer upgrade (2-3 years).

How do I check my transformer capacity?

Call our emergency line (8301849474) with your exact location. We check KSEB portal data and give you real-time capacity status within 24 hours.

Is SBI 10-year loan really better than 5-year?

Yes! 10-year loan has 42% lower monthly EMI (₹1,450 vs ₹2,500 for 3kW). This makes solar affordable for most families without budget pressure.

What if my transformer is already at 90%?

We check for alternative transformers in your area. If none available, you'll need to wait 2-3 years. This is why checking NOW is critical.

How long does emergency installation take?

With fast-track processing: 3-5 days after capacity confirmation. Without urgency: 2-3 weeks.

Can I get both subsidy and SBI loan?

Yes! 40% subsidy reduces your cost, then SBI loan finances the remaining 60% at 6% interest for 10 years.
