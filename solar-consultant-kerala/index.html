Our Solar Consulting Services

Before investing capital into a solar PV system, it is critical to engineer a solution that maximizes energy yield (kWh/kWp). Our engineering team provides data-driven consulting to ensure maximum ROI.

Energy & Load Auditing

We analyze your historical KSEB billing data and peak load requirements to size the exact solar PV capacity needed to offset your OPEX without over-investing.

3D Shadow Analysis

Using advanced software, we map your roof structure against the sun's path across Kerala to identify micro-shadows from trees or adjacent buildings, preventing generation losses.

Financial ROI Modeling

We provide comprehensive financial breakdowns including payback periods, Accelerated Depreciation (AD) benefits for businesses, and KSEB net-metering offsets.

KSEB Feasibility Check

Not all local transformers can support commercial solar loads. We conduct a technical feasibility check with the local KSEB section office before you commit to the project.

Who Needs a Solar Consultant?

Factories & Commercial

For high-consumption MSMEs, cashew units, and manufacturing plants looking to slash operational costs and utilize factory rooftops efficiently.

Hospitals & Schools

Institutions with consistent daytime load requirements. We help design systems that offset peak daytime grid usage.

Premium Villas

For large residential homes requiring complex architectural integration, multi-roof planning, and aesthetics without compromising generation.

Our 4-Step Engineering Process

1

Initial Site Survey & Drone Mapping

Our engineers visit your site to assess structural integrity, roof dimensions, and local grid connection points.

2

Technical Design & Proposal

We generate a detailed proposal including system capacity, tier-1 component selection (Tata Power/Adani/Enphase), and structural drawings.

3

Grid Approval & Statutory Clearances

We coordinate directly with KSEB and local authorities to secure all necessary capacity approvals before construction begins.

4

Execution & Commissioning

Our EPC team handles the complete installation, net-meter synchronization, and final handover with performance monitoring software.
