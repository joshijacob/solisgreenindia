5kW Solar System Specifications & Performance

Space Requirement

A 5kW setup requires approximately 400 to 500 sq. ft. of shadow-free rooftop space. We engineer custom elevated hot-dip galvanized structures for concrete terraces and pitched tile roofs.

Power Generation

On average, a 5kW solar system generates 20 to 25 units of electricity per day, adding up to roughly 600 – 750 units a month in Kerala conditions.

Return on Investment (ROI)

The payback period for a 5kW system is typically 3.2 to 3.8 years. After this, you enjoy virtually free electricity for the remaining 20+ years of the system's lifespan.

KSEB Net Metering

Excess power generated during peak daylight hours is exported to the KSEB grid. You utilize these banked units during the night, reducing your net bill to almost zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I claim the ₹78,000 subsidy for a 5kW plant?

Solis Green manages your complete PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana portal registration, KSEB feasibility verification, and commissioning documentation. The central subsidy of ₹78,000 is credited directly into your bank account via DBT.

Will the system work during power cuts?

An on-grid solar system will shut down automatically during KSEB grid outages for linemen safety (anti-islanding). For uninterrupted power during outages, we offer hybrid solar systems with lithium or tubular battery storage.

Can I install a 5kW system on a sloped tile roof?

Yes! We use specialized anodized aluminum clamps and elevated hot-dip galvanized mounting structures that bolt securely without drilling or damaging your roof tiles.

What warranty is included?

We provide a 25-to-30-year performance warranty on Tier-1 TOPCon solar panels, a 5-to-10-year manufacturer warranty on the inverter, and 5 years of complimentary local Annual Maintenance (AMC).
