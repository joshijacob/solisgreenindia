What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar is a Government of India rooftop solar subsidy scheme managed by MNRE. It supports residential consumers by reducing the upfront cost of on-grid solar systems. The subsidy amount is credited directly to the homeowner’s bank account after installation and inspection.

The scheme is fully applicable across Pathanamthitta district, subject to KSEB feasibility and transformer capacity.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Amount (Pathanamthitta)

1kW – 2kW System

Subsidy up to ₹30,000

3kW Solar System

Subsidy up to ₹78,000

Above 3kW

Maximum subsidy capped at ₹78,000

⚠ Subsidy applies only for residential on-grid solar systems. Commercial and off-grid systems are not eligible.

Eligibility for Solar Subsidy in Pathanamthitta

  • Residential electricity connection (KSEB)
  • Property ownership or valid consent
  • Shadow-free rooftop space
  • Transformer capacity availability in your locality
  • On-grid solar system installation only

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Process (Step-by-Step)

1. Free Site Survey

Roof inspection and transformer capacity verification.

2. MNRE Application

Online registration and system approval.

3. Solar Installation

Installation by MNRE-approved solar vendor.

4. KSEB Net-Metering

Inspection and bi-directional meter installation.

5. Subsidy Credit

Subsidy credited directly to your bank account.

Documents Required for Solar Subsidy

  • Latest KSEB electricity bill
  • Aadhaar & ID proof
  • Property ownership document
  • Cancelled cheque / bank details
  • Solar installation photographs

Important Local Notes for Pathanamthitta

Subsidy timelines may vary across locations such as Thiruvalla, Adoor, Ranni, Pandalam, Mallappally and Kozhencherry due to transformer constraints. Professional coordination with KSEB is critical to avoid delays or rejection.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy – FAQs (Pathanamthitta)

How long does subsidy approval take?

Usually 30–60 days after KSEB inspection and net-meter installation.

Is subsidy available for battery or hybrid systems?

No. Subsidy applies only to on-grid rooftop solar systems.

Can I apply for subsidy myself?

Yes, but professional handling reduces rejection and delays.
