Frequently Asked Questions — Solar in Pathanamthitta

How much solar subsidy can I get in Pathanamthitta under PM Surya Ghar?

Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers receive a direct bank subsidy of ₹33,000 for 1kW, ₹66,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW or higher capacities. As an approved MNRE vendor, Solis Green submits all documents on the national portal so the subsidy is deposited directly into your bank account after KSEB commissioning.

What is the realistic price of a 3kW solar system in Pathanamthitta?

A high-quality 3kW on-grid solar plant using Tier-1 TOPCon bifacial panels and Solis/Growatt string inverters costs approximately ₹1,95,000 gross. With the ₹78,000 subsidy credited back, your net cost is only ₹1,17,000 to ₹1,27,000. It produces 360–450 units monthly, bringing ₹0 electricity bills for typical 3BHK homes.

How long does KSEB net-metering approval take?

Once your solar plant is physically erected, Solis Green submits the work completion report, test report, and KSEB scheme approval request. In Pathanamthitta district, the local section office typically inspects the installation and installs the bi-directional net meter within 7 to 14 working days.

Can solar panels survive heavy monsoon winds and rain in Pathanamthitta?

Yes. All Solis Green structures utilize 80-micron hot-dip galvanized or anodized architectural aluminum engineered for wind loads up to 160 km/h. High-grade IP68 weather-sealed junction boxes and class-A lightning arrestors with independent chemical earth pits protect against lightning storms common in eastern Pathanamthitta.
