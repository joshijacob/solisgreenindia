About The Role

Solis Green Energy Solutions, an authorized Tata Power Solar Channel Partner and Kerala's trusted solar EPC installer, is seeking a motivated, energetic Solar Sales Person to expand our residential and commercial rooftop solar presence in Thiruvalla and surrounding areas (covering Pathanamthitta, Kottayam, and Alappuzha borders).

Whether you have direct sales experience or you are a confident fresher eager to build a lucrative career in the green energy sector, this is an outstanding opportunity to join a fast-growing company with attractive earnings and full training support.

Who Can Apply (Eligibility)

  • Good communication skills: Fluency in Malayalam with decent English to effectively present solar solutions to homeowners and business clients.
  • Willingness to travel locally: Comfortable traveling within Thiruvalla and nearby localities for customer visits and site surveys. (Own two-wheeler preferred).
  • Experience: Prior experience in direct sales, customer interaction, FMCG, banking, or insurance is preferred, but freshers with great enthusiasm and hunger to learn can also apply!
  • Attitude: Self-motivated, target-oriented, customer-friendly, and proactive mindset.

Key Responsibilities

  • Meet prospective residential and commercial clients in Thiruvalla and adjacent regions.
  • Explain the financial benefits, KSEB electricity bill savings, and Central Government subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (up to ₹78,000 subsidy).
  • Conduct initial site visits and capture rooftop photographs/details for technical quotation.
  • Present proposals, resolve customer questions, follow up, and close rooftop solar installation orders.
  • Coordinate with the engineering and installation teams for smooth project commissioning.
  • Achieve monthly sales targets and earn high performance commissions.

What We Offer

  • Guaranteed Monthly Package: ₹20,000/- fixed per month.
  • Travel Allowance: Separate reimbursement for official local travel.
  • Uncapped Incentives: Substantial extra incentive bonus on every closed solar installation.
  • Comprehensive Training: On-the-job mentorship on solar technology, solar ROI calculations, and sales closing techniques.
  • Fast Career Growth: Opportunity to grow into Team Leader / Senior Sales Manager roles as our operations expand.
