Frequently Asked Questions — Solar Cost in Kerala

What is the average solar installation cost in Kerala in 2026?

For a standard 3kW on-grid home system, the turnkey installation cost is approximately ₹1,95,000 gross. After deducting the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar central subsidy, your effective net investment is approximately ₹1,17,000.

What components are included in this cost?

The turnkey cost includes Tier-1 DCR TOPCon bifacial panels, a high-efficiency on-grid string inverter with WiFi monitoring, hot-dip galvanized or anodized aluminum structures, DCDB/ACDB boxes with Type II SPDs, pure copper cabling, dual chemical earthings, and complete KSEB net-metering liaison.

Can I finance the solar installation cost through bank loans?

Yes. Nationalized banks including SBI and Canara Bank offer collateral-free solar loans starting at 6% to 7% per annum under PM Surya Ghar. For a 3kW plant, the monthly EMI of around ₹1,950 is significantly lower than your monthly electricity savings.
