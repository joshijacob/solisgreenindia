Frequently Asked Questions About Solar in Kerala

How much subsidy can I get for solar panels in Kerala?

Homeowners in Kerala can get up to ₹78,000 government subsidy under the MNRE solar scheme for on-grid systems. Solis Green handles the complete subsidy and KSEB approval process.

Is solar profitable for homes in Thiruvalla?

Yes. Thiruvalla has excellent solar potential. Most homes save ₹3,500–₹9,000 per month depending on system size, with a payback period of 3–4 years.

Which solar system size is best for my house?

A 3kW system is ideal for most 2–3 BHK homes. Larger homes or commercial buildings may require 5kW or above. We offer a free site survey to recommend the right system.

Do you provide solar installation across Kerala?

Yes. Solis Green Energy Solutions provides solar installation services across Pathanamthitta, Kottayam, Alappuzha, Kollam and nearby districts.

Do you help with KSEB net metering and approvals?

Yes. We manage the complete process including KSEB net metering, inspections, subsidy documentation and bank loan assistance.
