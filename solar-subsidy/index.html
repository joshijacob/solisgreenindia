Understanding the Solar Subsidy Scheme

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen residing in Kerala
  • Ownership of the roof/property
  • Existing KSEB electricity connection
  • System size between 1kW and 10kW
  • Installation by MNRE-approved channel partner (like us)

Subsidy Amount

  • 1kW: ₹30,000
  • 2kW: ₹60,000
  • 3kW: ₹78,000
  • 4kW to 10kW: ₹78,000 (fixed)
  • Maximum subsidy: ₹78,000

Documents Required

  • Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
  • Proof of address (electricity bill)
  • Property ownership documents
  • Recent passport size photo
  • Bank account details (for DBT)

Processing Timeline

  • Application submission: 1 day
  • DISCOM approval: 7-10 days
  • Installation: 2-5 days
  • Inspection & net meter: 7 days
  • Subsidy release: 15-30 days post-installation

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for solar subsidy?

Any resident of Kerala with a rooftop suitable for solar, owning the property, and having a KSEB connection. Residential, institutional, and social sectors are eligible. Commercial/industrial are not.

How much subsidy can I get?

1kW: ₹30,000, 2kW: ₹60,000, 3kW and above: ₹78,000 (maximum). The subsidy is fixed per kW as per MNRE guidelines.

How is the subsidy paid?

Through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to your bank account after the system is installed and inspected by KSEB.

Can I apply for subsidy myself?

Yes, but the process is complex. We assist as an MNRE-approved channel partner, ensuring correct documentation and faster approval.

How long does it take to get subsidy?

Typically 30-45 days after installation, depending on KSEB inspection and processing.

Is subsidy available for commercial buildings?

No, the 40% subsidy is only for residential, institutional, and social sectors. Commercial can avail other benefits like accelerated depreciation.
