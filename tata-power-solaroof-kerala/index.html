Solar System Components

A Tata Power Solaroof system is a symphony of high-quality components working together:

  • Inverter: The brain of the system, converting DC into usable AC electricity.

Solar Panel Technologies

Tata Power utilizes cutting-edge photovoltaic technologies. The two primary types of panels used in our Rooftop Solar Kerala projects are:

  • Mono PERC (Passivated Emitter and Rear Cell): These panels offer higher efficiency, particularly in low-light and high-temperature conditions. They have a sleek, uniform dark appearance and require less roof space per kilowatt.
  • Bifacial Modules: These advanced panels can absorb sunlight from both the front and the rear side (capturing reflected light from the roof surface), increasing overall generation yield by up to 15%.

Micro Inverters vs String Inverters

The choice of inverter dictates how efficiently your system handles shading.

String Inverters

In a standard setup, panels are connected in a "string" and fed into a single central inverter. If a shadow falls on one panel, the output of the entire string drops. This is cost-effective and highly efficient for unshaded, flat roofs.

Micro Inverters

For roofs with complex architecture or shading from trees (very common in Kerala), micro inverters are attached to the back of each individual panel. This means each panel operates independently. If one panel is shaded, the rest of the array continues generating at full capacity. Solis Green Energy is an expert in deploying advanced micro-inverter architecture alongside Tata Power panels.

Battery Storage

With frequent power outages in certain rural areas, battery storage becomes essential. Modern Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries offer deep discharge capabilities, longer lifespans (up to 10 years), and require zero maintenance compared to traditional lead-acid tubular batteries.

Hybrid Solar, On Grid Solar, and Off Grid Solar

Understanding the architecture types is crucial for making the right investment:

  • On Grid Solar: Connects directly to the KSEB grid. It is the cheapest and most common system, designed to maximize Solar Savings. However, it shuts down during a power cut for safety reasons (anti-islanding).
  • Off Grid Solar: Completely independent of the KSEB grid. It relies heavily on battery banks. Suitable for remote locations where grid connection is impossible.
  • Hybrid Solar: The ultimate solution. A Hybrid Solar system provides the bill-reducing benefits of net metering while also featuring a battery bank to provide uninterrupted power during grid failures.

Net Metering Process in Kerala

Net Metering Kerala is governed by KSEB regulations. The process involves:

  1. Applying for technical feasibility with the local KSEB section office.
  2. Paying the registration fee.
  3. Receiving the feasibility clearance (valid for a specific period).
  4. Executing the installation.
  5. Submitting the completion report and testing certificates to the Electrical Inspectorate (if applicable) and KSEB.
  6. KSEB officials inspecting the site and installing the bi-directional Net Meter.
  7. Signing the power purchase agreement.

We manage this entire bureaucratic pipeline so you don't have to visit a single government office.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the lifespan of a Tata Power Solaroof system?

Tata Power solar panels are designed to last for more than 25 years. They come with a 25-year linear performance warranty guaranteeing high output for decades.

2. Is Tata Power Solaroof eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Yes, Tata Power modules are DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) compliant and ALMM approved, making them fully eligible for the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy.

3. How much roof space is required for a 3kW system?

A standard 3kW system requires approximately 300 square feet of shadow-free roof area.

4. Do I need battery backup with an On-Grid system?

No, an On-Grid system uses the KSEB grid as a virtual battery through net metering. However, it will not provide power during a grid outage.

5. Will solar panels work during the Kerala monsoons?

Yes, solar panels generate electricity from ambient daylight, not just direct sunlight. While generation is lower during heavy rain, Tata Power's high-efficiency panels still produce significant power.

6. Are the mounting structures rust-proof?

We use Hot-Dip Galvanized Iron (HDGI) or Aluminum structures, which are highly resistant to rust and corrosion, specifically engineered for Kerala's humid climate.

7. Can I expand my solar system later?

Yes, it is possible. However, the existing inverter must have the capacity to handle additional panels, or a new inverter (like micro inverters) must be added.

8. How do I apply for a Solar Loan in Kerala?

We assist you with the entire documentation process. Once the KSEB feasibility is approved, you can submit the quote and KSEB documents to banks like SBI or Union Bank for quick loan disbursement.

9. What happens if I generate more electricity than I use?

Through Net Metering, excess units are exported to KSEB and credited to your account. These credits offset your usage during the night or cloudy days.

10. How often should I clean the solar panels?

In Kerala, it is recommended to wash the panels with clean water every 2 to 4 weeks depending on the dust levels in your area.

11. Is a site survey really necessary?

Absolutely. A site survey determines roof strength, shading from nearby trees or buildings, and electrical routing, which are critical for an accurate proposal.

12. Do commercial installations get the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

No, the subsidy is strictly for residential consumers. Commercial entities benefit through tax savings via accelerated depreciation.

13. What is the difference between Mono PERC and Polycrystalline panels?

Mono PERC panels are newer technology, more efficient, and perform better in high heat and low light compared to older Polycrystalline panels.

14. Do you serve areas outside Thiruvalla and Pathanamthitta?

Yes, as a premier Solar EPC in Kerala, we serve Kottayam, Alappuzha, Kollam, and surrounding regions comprehensively.

15. Can I run my air conditioner on a solar system?

Yes, an appropriately sized system (like 5kW) can easily offset the electricity consumption of multiple air conditioners.

16. Are there any hidden costs in the installation?

No. Solis Green Energy provides transparent pricing. Any requirement for extra cabling or civil work structure elevation is clearly mentioned in the initial proposal.

17. What is Anti-Islanding protection?

It is a safety feature in On-Grid inverters that immediately stops exporting power to the grid during a blackout, protecting linemen working on the grid.

18. Do solar panels damage the roof?

When installed by professionals like us, solar panels do not damage the roof. In fact, they protect the roof directly beneath them from sun and rain.

19. Can I install solar panels on a sloping tile roof?

Yes, we have specialized mounting brackets designed specifically to interlock with Kerala’s traditional sloping tile roofs without causing leaks.

20. How is the subsidy amount credited?

21. Why should I choose a Hybrid system?

If your area experiences frequent and prolonged power cuts, a Hybrid system with battery backup ensures you have uninterrupted electricity.

22. What happens to solar panels after 25 years?

They continue to generate electricity, but at a slightly reduced efficiency (around 80% of original capacity). They can also be safely recycled.

23. Is lightning protection included?

Yes, all our installations include dedicated Lightning Arrestors (LA) and separate earthing pits to protect the system and your home.

24. Can apartment builders install centralized solar?

Yes, apartment builders can install large systems to power common areas (lifts, water pumps, lighting), drastically reducing association maintenance fees.
