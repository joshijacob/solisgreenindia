Solar Installation Reality in Alappuzha

Solar installations in Alappuzha require special planning due to coastal humidity, salt exposure, roof types, and transformer feasibility.

  • Corrosion-resistant structures are essential near coastal & backwater areas
  • Transformer capacity verification is important in town limits
  • Sloped & tiled roofs need customised mounting
  • Many homes start with 3kW and upgrade later

What Most Installers Don’t Explain

  • Low-quality materials fail faster in coastal climates
  • Improper earthing causes inverter damage
  • Subsidy delays occur due to paperwork errors
  • KSEB approval timelines vary by section
