Solar Installation Reality in Mavelikkara

Solar installations in Mavelikkara require careful planning due to mixed residential layouts, old tiled roofs, transformer availability, and phased system upgrades.

  • Transformer capacity verification is crucial in town limits
  • Older tiled roofs need customised mounting structures
  • Most homes start with 3kW and expand to 5kW later
  • Hybrid systems are popular due to frequent short power cuts

What Many Installers Don’t Tell You

  • Improper earthing causes inverter failure
  • Low-quality structures reduce system lifespan
  • Incorrect paperwork delays MNRE subsidy
  • Future expansion must be planned at design stage
