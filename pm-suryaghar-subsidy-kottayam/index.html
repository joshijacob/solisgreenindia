What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar (MNRE Rooftop Solar Scheme) is a Government of India initiative that provides financial subsidy to residential consumers installing on-grid rooftop solar systems. The subsidy is credited directly to the consumer’s bank account after successful installation and inspection.

This scheme is applicable across Kottayam district, subject to KSEB feasibility and transformer capacity.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Amount (Kottayam)

1kW – 2kW System

Subsidy up to ₹30,000

3kW Solar System

Subsidy up to ₹78,000

Above 3kW

Maximum subsidy capped at ₹78,000

⚠ Subsidy applies only for residential on-grid systems. Commercial systems are not eligible.

Eligibility for Solar Subsidy in Kottayam

  • Residential property owner
  • Valid KSEB electricity connection
  • Shadow-free roof area available
  • Transformer capacity available in your locality
  • On-grid solar system only (no battery subsidy)

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Process (Step-by-Step)

1. Site Survey & Feasibility

Roof inspection and KSEB transformer capacity check.

2. Design & Application

Online MNRE portal registration and system approval.

3. Installation

Solar plant installation by MNRE-approved vendor.

4. KSEB Inspection

Net-meter installation and final inspection.

5. Subsidy Credit

Subsidy amount credited directly to your bank account.

Documents Required for Solar Subsidy

  • Electricity bill (KSEB)
  • Aadhaar & ID proof
  • Property ownership proof
  • Cancelled cheque / bank details
  • Photographs of installation

Important Local Notes for Kottayam

Subsidy approval timelines and feasibility may vary based on location such as Changanacherry, Kanjirappally, Erattupetta, Pala and high-range areas. Transformer constraints are common in some regions — professional handling is critical.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy – FAQs

How long does subsidy approval take?

Typically 30–60 days after installation and KSEB inspection.

Is subsidy available for battery systems?

No. Subsidy applies only to on-grid rooftop solar systems.

Can I apply myself?

Yes, but professional handling avoids rejection and delays.
